Case Study

VGS Worldwide Limited

A full digital package — website, mobile app and social presence — built to support growth across locations.

The Challenge

VGS Worldwide Limited needed a digital presence that could keep up with a growing, multi-location business — a website that actually converted visitors into enquiries, a mobile app for customers, and a social media presence that matched the quality of the service on the ground.

The Solution

Jelvia Tech Studio delivered a complete package — a redesigned website, a customer-facing mobile app, and a social media strategy — all tied together under one consistent brand, built to scale as the business expanded into new locations.

The Results

  • 200% increase in online sales
  • 20,000+ app downloads
  • 150% boost in social media engagement
  • Expanded to 10 locations

Client Testimonial

"JELVIA TECH STUDIO transformed our digital presence. From our website to our mobile app and social media, everything is now professional and cohesive. Our online sales have tripled." — Adebayo Johnson, VGS Worldwide Limited
