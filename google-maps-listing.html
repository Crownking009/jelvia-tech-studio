Why This Matters

Especially for local, service-based businesses

A lot of the businesses we build websites for depend on being found locally — a restaurant, a cleaning service, a logistics company. If your Google Business Profile is missing, incomplete, or in the wrong category, potential customers searching nearby often can't find you at all, even with a great website.
